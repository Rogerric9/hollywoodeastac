Privacy Policy

Hollywood East Autographs & Collectibles respects your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or trade customer information.

If you contact us by email, we may use your name, email address, and message only to respond to your question or provide customer service.

If you place an order, your order information may be used to process your purchase, arrange shipping, answer questions about your order, or handle customer service matters.

Payment information is handled through PayPal. Hollywood East Autographs & Collectibles does not receive or store your full credit card number or PayPal password.

This website may use basic browser technology, such as local storage, to keep track of items placed in your shopping cart while you browse the site.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hollyeastac@gmail.com.
