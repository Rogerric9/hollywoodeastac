Wyrmspire Hall: Official Rules

Last updated: November 13, 2026

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A purchase will not improve your chances of winning. This is a game of skill: prizes are earned by solving the game's puzzles, not by chance.

2. Who may take part

  • The Promotion is open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are at least 13 years old when they create their account.
  • Players aged 13 to 17 must have the permission of a parent or legal guardian to take part and to claim a prize. The Sponsor may ask for that permission to be confirmed before a prize is sent.

3. Promotion period

The Promotion is open now and continues until the Sponsor ends it. The Sponsor may end, suspend or change the Promotion at any time, as described in section 10.

4. How to take part

  1. Visit hollywoodeastac.com/wyrmspire.html and create a free account with an avatar name, an email address and a password.
  2. Play the game and break all ten spells in Wyrmspire Hall.
  3. When you finish, a prize selection screen appears. Choose one of the prizes shown and enter your full name and United States mailing address.

There is no fee to play and nothing to buy. The game is played entirely in your web browser.

5. Prizes

  • Every eligible player who completes the game may choose one prize from those shown on the prize selection screen at the time they finish.

  • Each person may use only one account. Prizes claimed through extra accounts, false information or any other attempt to get around these limits may be cancelled.

Prizes are mailed to the United States address given at the time of the claim, normally within 30 days. The Sponsor is not responsible for prizes lost or delayed because of an incorrect or incomplete address, or for delays caused by the postal service.

Completing the game is checked by the game's own records, and the Sponsor's decision on whether a game was completed fairly is final.

Your email address is used for your game account (for example, to help you recover a forgotten password). Your name and mailing address are collected only when you claim a prize and are used only to send it. We do not sell your information. See our Privacy Policy for more.
