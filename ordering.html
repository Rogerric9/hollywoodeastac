Ordering & Payment

Most items offered by Hollywood East Autographs & Collectibles are one-of-a-kind items. Unless a product page specifically says otherwise, only one of each item is available.

Orders are handled through PayPal checkout. You do not need a PayPal account to complete a purchase if PayPal offers a debit or credit card checkout option.

Shipping, handling, and final payment details will be confirmed during PayPal checkout.

Authentication information, condition notes, and other important details are shown on each product detail page when available.

Please review product photos and descriptions carefully before placing an order. If you have a question about an item, please contact us before purchasing.
