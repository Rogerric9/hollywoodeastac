Nice 8x10 B&W 8x10 photo signed by 3 of Honeymooners

Full Description

This is a rare item indeed. A beautiful 8x10 B&W photograph signed by Art Carney (Ed Norton), Audrey Meadows (Alice Kramden), and Joyce Randolph (Trixie Norton). Carney’s portrayal of Ed Norton earned him five Emmy Awards, and he later won the Academy Award for Best Actor for Harry and Tonto (1974), a rare leap from beloved television sidekick to Oscar-winning film star. Meadows (1922–1996) brought sharp comic timing to Alice Kramden opposite Jackie Gleason, becoming one of classic television’s most memorable wives. Randolph, who died in 2024 at age 99, was the last surviving member of the show’s core cast, and her gum-chewing Trixie remains one of The Honeymooners’ most fondly remembered characters.
