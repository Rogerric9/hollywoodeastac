Full Description

(Deceased) Maureen O’Hara (August 17, 1920–October 24, 2015) was an Irish-American actress celebrated for her fiery screen presence and striking red hair. She starred in classic films including The Quiet Man, Miracle on 34th Street, How Green Was My Valley and The Parent Trap. O’Hara was especially known for her frequent collaborations with director John Ford and actor John Wayne.
