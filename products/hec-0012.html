Full Description

The Terry Baxter Orchestra was an American easy-listening studio ensemble known for polished instrumental and choral arrangements of popular songs. Recording primarily for Columbia Musical Treasuries and the Columbia Record Club during the late 1960s and 1970s, the orchestra released collections such as The Best of ’69, The Best of ’71, Song Sung Blue, and Yesterday: The Wonderful Music of the Beatles. The recordings typically combined lush strings, brass, and chorus in a smooth orchestral-pop style.
