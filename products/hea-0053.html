Full Description

Sophia Loren (born September 20, 1934) is an Italian actress and one of the most celebrated international film stars of Hollywood’s Golden Age. She won the Academy Award for Best Actress for her performance in Two Women, becoming the first performer to win an acting Oscar for a non-English-language role. Her other notable films include Houseboat, Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow, Marriage Italian Style, El Cid, Grumpier Old Men, and The Life Ahead. She also received an Honorary Academy Award for her contributions to world cinema.
