Full Description

Dolenz, Jones, Boyce & Hart was a pop-rock group formed in the mid-1970s by former Monkees Micky Dolenz and Davy Jones with songwriters and performers Tommy Boyce and Bobby Hart. Boyce and Hart had written and produced many of the Monkees’ early recordings. The quartet released a self-titled album in 1976 and toured internationally, performing both new material and familiar Monkees songs.
