Full Description

(Deceased) Bo Schembechler (born April 1, 1929 – died November 17, 2006) was an American college football coach best known for leading the University of Michigan Wolverines from 1969 to 1989. During his tenure, Michigan won or shared 13 Big Ten Conference championships and became one of the nation’s most successful football programs. Schembechler was renowned for his intense leadership, disciplined teams, and famous rivalry with Ohio State coach Woody Hayes in the annual “Ten-Year War.”
