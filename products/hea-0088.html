Full Description

(Deceased) Danny Aiello (June 20, 1933 – December 12, 2019) was an American actor best known for portraying Sal, the pizzeria owner in Spike Lee’s Do the Right Thing, a performance that earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor. His many other film credits included The Godfather Part II, Once Upon a Time in America, The Purple Rose of Cairo, Moonstruck, Harlem Nights, Hudson Hawk, and Léon: The Professional. Aiello was known for playing tough, streetwise characters while bringing warmth, humor, and vulnerability to his roles.
