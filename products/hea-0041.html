Full Description

Juliet Prowse (Deceased; September 25, 1936 – September 14, 1996) was a dancer, actress, and singer known for her work in film, television, stage productions, and Las Vegas shows. Born in Bombay and raised in South Africa, she gained international attention in the musical Can-Can and later starred opposite Elvis Presley in G.I. Blues. Prowse also appeared in numerous television programs and stage musicals, earning recognition for her dancing ability, elegance, and distinctive screen presence.
