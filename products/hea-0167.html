Gary Lewis (born July 31, 1945) is an American singer, musician and drummer best known as the leader of the 1960s pop group Gary Lewis & the Playboys. The son of entertainer Jerry Lewis, he sang lead on hits including “This Diamond Ring,” “Count Me In,” “Save Your Heart for Me,” “Everybody Loves a Clown” and “She’s Just My Style.” The group became one of the era’s most successful American pop acts, placing seven consecutive singles in the Top 10.
