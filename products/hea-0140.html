Full Description

(Deceased) George Foreman (January 10, 1949 – March 21, 2025) was an American professional boxer, Olympic gold medalist, minister, author, and businessman. Known as “Big George,” he twice won the world heavyweight championship and became the oldest heavyweight champion in history when he regained the title at age 45. Outside the ring, Foreman achieved worldwide fame as the spokesperson for the George Foreman Grill and became one of boxing’s most recognizable and popular personalities.
