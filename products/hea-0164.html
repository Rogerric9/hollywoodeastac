Full Description

Tommy Reynolds was an American singer and multi-instrumentalist best known as an original member of the soft-rock group Hamilton, Joe Frank & Reynolds. He previously performed with the T-Bones and later became the lead singer of Shango. With Hamilton, Joe Frank & Reynolds, he helped record the 1971 hit “Don’t Pull Your Love” before leaving the group in the early 1970s.
