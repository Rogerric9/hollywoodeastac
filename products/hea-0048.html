Full Description

Jean Simmons (Deceased; January 31, 1929 – January 22, 2010) was a British-American actress whose distinguished career spanned more than six decades. She received Academy Award nominations for Hamlet and The Happy Ending and starred in such notable films as Great Expectations, Guys and Dolls, The Robe, Spartacus, Elmer Gantry, and The Big Country. Simmons was admired for her elegance, versatility, and memorable performances in both British and Hollywood productions.
