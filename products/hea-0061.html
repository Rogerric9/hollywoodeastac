Full Description

(Deceased) Lloyd Bridges (January 15, 1913 – March 10, 1998) was an American film and television actor whose career spanned more than six decades. He became widely known as underwater adventurer Mike Nelson in the television series Sea Hunt and later appeared in such films as High Noon, Sahara, Little Big Horn, Airplane!, Hot Shots! and Blown Away. Bridges successfully moved between drama, westerns, adventure stories and comedy, earning particular praise for his deadpan comic performances later in life. He was the father of actors Beau Bridges and Jeff Bridges.
