Full Description

Olivia Newton-John (Deceased) was a British-Australian singer and actress who became one of the most successful female entertainers of the 1970s and 1980s. Born on September 26, 1948, in Cambridge, England, she moved with her family to Australia as a child and began performing professionally while still young. Newton-John first achieved international success as a pop and country singer with hits including "If Not for You," "Let Me Be There," "I Honestly Love You," and "Have You Never Been Mellow." Her warm voice and wholesome image made her a major recording star. Her career reached another level in 1978 when she starred opposite John Travolta in the movie musical "Grease." As Sandy Olsson, Newton-John performed some of the film's most memorable songs, including "Hopelessly Devoted to You," "Summer Nights," and "You're the One That I Want." "Grease" became one of the most successful movie musicals of all time. She continued her success with songs such as "A Little More Love," "Magic," and the 1981 hit "Physical," which became one of the biggest singles of the decade. Newton-John remained active in music, film, and charitable work for many years. She died on August 8, 2022, at age 73. Her recordings and her performance in "Grease" remain an enduring part of popular culture.
