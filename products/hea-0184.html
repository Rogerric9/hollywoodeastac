Full Description

Kathy Garver (born December 13, 1945) is an American actress and voice artist best known for playing Catherine “Cissy” Davis on the television series Family Affair. She also appeared in films and television programs beginning as a child actress and later became a prolific voice performer in animated series and commercials. Garver has continued acting, writing and making appearances celebrating classic television.
